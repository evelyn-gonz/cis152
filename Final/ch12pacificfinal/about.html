Our Story

When Linda and Steven Hubert first set eyes on what would become the Pacific Trails Resort, they knew they had found something special. For 26 years, Linda and Steven have worked tirelessly to make sure their resort is a place where people from all over can relax, take time off from everyday life and enjoy nature.

Over the years, Pacific Trails Resort has evolved and become a landmark where thousands of visitors congregate and enjoy the beautiful scenery together. Activities like kayaking, hiking and bird watching bring visitors together and provide entertainment away from the bright city lights that surround them.

Whether you are looking for a solo weekend getaway, or a much needed vacation with the family, Pacific Trails Resort offers a customizable experience where guests can explore the terrain on their own terms.
